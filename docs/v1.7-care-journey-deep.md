# v1.7 Complete Care Journey — Detailed Build

## Goal
Connect consultation through treatment planning and recovery into one understandable patient journey.

## Deliverables
- Care plan model
- Journey stages and status
- Treatment estimate model
- Care coordinator workflow
- Hospital/provider referral boundary
- Procedure planning
- Travel coordination boundary
- Medication fulfillment boundary where legally permitted
- Recovery milestones
- Follow-up scheduling

## Safety
Clinical decisions remain with licensed clinicians. Estimates are informational and must show currency, assumptions and expiration. Medication and procedure fulfillment require jurisdiction-specific legal and clinical review. No guarantee of savings, outcomes or eligibility.

## Patient UX
Timeline-first experience showing current stage, next action, responsible party, expected timing, documents needed and cost estimate. Include clear distinction between medical advice, operational coordination and financial estimates.

## Tests
Validate stage transitions, ownership, expired estimates, missing required documents and unauthorized coordinator access.
