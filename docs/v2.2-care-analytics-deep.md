# v2.2 Care Analytics — Detailed Build

## Goal
Give operations and care teams useful aggregate metrics without exposing unnecessary patient-level clinical data.

## Deliverables
- Consultation volume
- Completion/cancellation rate
- Appointment lead time
- Follow-up completion
- Doctor utilization
- Care journey stage counts
- Document processing metrics
- Operational dashboard
- Date-range and cohort filters

## Privacy
Analytics should use aggregate or de-identified data wherever possible. Restrict patient-level drill-down to explicitly authorized operational workflows. Do not expose clinical notes or prescription contents in aggregate dashboards.

## Tests
Timezone/date-range correctness, authorization, aggregation correctness, empty periods, pagination, and privacy field minimization.
