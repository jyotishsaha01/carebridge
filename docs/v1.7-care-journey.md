# v1.7 End-to-End Care Journey

## Scope
- Treatment-plan coordination
- Transparent treatment estimates
- Cross-border care planning
- Hospital/operation coordination
- Medication fulfillment where legally permitted
- Travel/logistics coordination where applicable
- Post-treatment follow-up
- Care-team communication

## Principle
CareBridge provides coordination and access to licensed professionals; clinical decisions remain with qualified treating clinicians and applicable local regulations.
