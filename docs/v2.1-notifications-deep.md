# v2.1 Notifications — Detailed Build

## Goal
Deliver reliable, preference-aware patient and doctor notifications without duplicate or unsafe messaging.

## Deliverables
- Notification preference model
- In-app notification inbox
- Email adapter boundary
- Appointment reminder scheduling
- Consultation-ready notification
- Document/prescription ready events
- Follow-up reminders
- Idempotency/deduplication keys
- Delivery status tracking
- Quiet hours/preferences

## Safety
Do not include sensitive clinical details in email/push previews. Use minimal notification text and route users to authenticated views. Never expose credentials or medical documents through notifications.

## Tests
Deduplication, preference suppression, quiet hours, retry behavior, invalid recipient handling and authorization.
