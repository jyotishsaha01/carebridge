# v1.5 Doctor Platform

## Scope
- Doctor onboarding
- Credential/verification workflow
- Availability and calendar
- Appointment queue
- Patient intake review
- Consultation workspace
- Secure video join
- Clinical notes
- Prescription authoring
- Follow-up plan
- Consultation completion

## Safety
Clinical actions require authenticated doctor identity, role authorization, assignment/appointment checks, immutable audit events, and clear draft vs signed states.
