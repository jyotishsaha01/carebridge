# CareBridge Product Roadmap

## v1.3 — Video Provider
- Provider-neutral adapter
- Secure short-lived participant tokens
- Patient/Doctor waiting room
- Active call controls
- Session audit events
- Production provider selection after compliance review

## v1.4 — Payments
- Pricing and currency display
- Consultation checkout
- Payment-provider adapter
- Payment intent/status webhooks
- Refund/cancellation rules
- Receipts
- Never store card numbers/CVV

## v1.5 — Doctor Platform
- Doctor onboarding and verification
- Availability/calendar
- Appointment queue
- Patient intake review
- Video consultation workspace
- Clinical notes
- Prescription
- Follow-up plan
- Consultation completion

## v1.6 — Admin Platform
- User/doctor management
- Doctor verification workflow
- Appointment operations
- Payment/refund operations
- Content/configuration management
- Audit logs
- Support tooling

## v1.7 — Care Journey
- Cross-border care planning
- Treatment estimates
- Medical travel coordination
- Hospital/operation workflow
- Medication fulfillment where legally permitted
- Post-treatment follow-up

## v2.0 — Production Readiness
- Production identity provider
- Secrets management
- Encryption/key management
- Object storage
- Observability/alerts
- Backup/recovery
- Security testing
- Privacy/compliance review
- Disaster recovery

All clinical development environments use synthetic data until production security and compliance controls are approved.
